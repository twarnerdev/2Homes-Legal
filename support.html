Requirements

TwoHomes requires iPhone or iPad running iOS 17.0 or later, and an internet connection to sync with your co-parent.

Getting Started

  1. Create an account with your email address, or sign in with Google.
  2. Add your children so schedules and expenses can be tracked per child.
  3. Set up your custody schedule to define your rotation.
  4. Set up your payment schedule for child support, alimony, and how you split shared expenses.
  5. Invite your co-parent using an invite code, so you both see the same information.

Linking With Your Co-Parent

How invite codes work

TwoHomes generates an invite code for your account. Your co-parent enters that code in their app to link the two accounts. Once linked, you share custody schedules, calendar events, expenses, payments, and the activity feed.

To share your code

Go to More → Settings → Co-Parent. Your invite code appears there with a Copy button. Send it to your co-parent however you like.

To enter your co-parent's code

Go to More → Settings → Co-Parent and tap Enter Partner's Code. You can also do this during onboarding when you first sign up.

"Invalid or expired invite code"

Each invite code can only be used once. If your co-parent's code has already been used, or if it was typed incorrectly, you'll see this message. Ask them to open Settings and send you the code currently shown there. Codes are not case-sensitive, but every character matters.

You cannot link with yourself

Entering your own invite code will not work. Make sure you are entering the code from your co-parent's account, not the one displayed in your own Settings.

Unlinking

Go to More → Settings → Co-Parent and tap Unlink Partner. This removes the connection for both of you. Shared records that were already created are not deleted.

Subscription and Billing

What it costs

Every new account gets 30 days of full access free — no payment method, nothing to cancel. After that, TwoHomes Premium is $49.99 per year per parent, purchased through the App Store. You are never charged unless you choose to subscribe.

Each parent needs their own subscription

Subscriptions are billed per parent, not per family. Your co-parent will need their own subscription on their own Apple Account to use the app. Linking with a subscribed co-parent does not give you access.

How much of my free access is left?

A banner on the Home screen shows the days remaining. You can also see your status under Settings → Subscription (Settings is the gear icon on the Home screen), and subscribe from there at any time.

My free access ended. What happens to my data?

Nothing is deleted. Your schedules, expenses, messages, and records are kept and are available again the moment you subscribe. Until then you can still restore a purchase, manage your Apple subscription, read our legal documents, or delete your account from the subscription screen.

Can I get another free access period?

No. Each person gets one 30-day free access period. It does not restart if you reinstall the app, sign in on another device, or delete and recreate your account.

Cancelling

Subscriptions are managed by Apple, not by us. Tap Manage Subscription under Settings → Subscription, or go to the iOS Settings app → tap your name → Subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before your renewal date to avoid being charged for the next period. Cancelling stops future charges; you keep access until the end of the period you already paid for.

I subscribed but the app still asks me to pay

Tap Restore Purchases on the subscription screen, or under Settings → Subscription. Make sure you are signed in to the same Apple Account you used to subscribe. If it still doesn't work, email us.

Refunds

All purchases are processed by Apple, so we cannot issue refunds directly. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com. If you believe you were charged in error, email us and we'll help you sort it out with Apple.

"There's a billing problem"

Apple could not charge your payment method. Update your payment details in the iOS Settings app → tap your name → Payment & Shipping. You keep access while Apple retries.

Notifications

TwoHomes sends notifications for schedule changes, swap requests, new expenses, and upcoming payments. If you aren't receiving them:

  • Open the iOS Settings app → TwoHomes → Notifications and confirm Allow Notifications is on.
  • Check that Focus or Do Not Disturb is not silencing them.
  • Confirm you are linked with your co-parent. Most notifications are triggered by their actions.
  • Try signing out and back in, which re-registers your device.

If you declined the notification prompt when you first installed the app, the only way to re-enable it is through the iOS Settings app.

Common Questions

My co-parent's changes aren't showing up

TwoHomes syncs through the internet. Check your connection, then pull down to refresh or reopen the app. Confirm you are still linked under More → Settings → Co-Parent.

Can I change the custody schedule after setting it up?

Yes. Open the Custody tab to adjust your schedule, apply one-off overrides for specific days, and request swaps with your co-parent.

How do swap requests work?

You request a change for a specific date and your co-parent approves or declines it. Approved swaps update the schedule for both of you. Past requests are visible in your swap history.

Can I get a record of custody or payments for court?

Yes. Open More → Reports to generate custody and payment reports that you can save or share.

I forgot my password

Tap Forgot Password on the sign-in screen and we'll email you a reset link. If you originally signed up with Google, use the Sign in with Google button instead; there is no separate TwoHomes password on that account.

Can I use TwoHomes if my co-parent won't?

Yes. You can track schedules, expenses, and payments on your own. Shared features such as swap requests and the activity feed require a linked co-parent.

Your Data

Export your data

Email privacy@twohomes.app from the address on your account and we will provide a copy of your data in a machine-readable format within 30 days.

Delete your account

Email support@twohomes.app from the address on your account and ask us to delete it. We remove your personal data within 30 days; deleted data may persist in encrypted backups for up to 90 days.

Deleting your TwoHomes account does not cancel your subscription. Cancel it separately in the iOS Settings app under Subscriptions, or you will continue to be billed by Apple.
